# Echo: The "Write Fast, Fix Later" AI Companion

## 1. Executive Summary
**Echo** is a cross-platform (Windows & Android) writing companion that eliminates the friction of real-time editing. By adopting a "dump now, polish later" philosophy, Echo allows users to type at the speed of thought without the distraction of grammar corrections or tone anxiety. With a single global shortcut (`Alt + Space` on Desktop) or a floating tap (Android), Echo instantly transforms messy, error-prone drafts into polished, tone-specific messages.

---

## 2. The Core Philosophy: "Permission to be Messy"
Unlike Grammarly or traditional editors that "nag" users with red underlines while they type, Echo embraces the raw, messy first draft. 
*   **Speed Over Perfection:** Focus on getting ideas out.
*   **Invisible Assistance:** No UI until you ask for it.
*   **Post-Processing Magic:** The transformation happens in-place, making the user look like a genius instantly.

---

## 3. Product Pillars
*   **The Echo Pulse:** A subtle, breathing indicator that lives near your cursor, signaling that Echo is ready to assist without being a distraction.
*   **The Echo Shimmer:** A signature horizontal light sweep animation that plays as messy text is replaced by polished prose.
*   **Tone Personalities:** Effortless switching between **Professional (👔)**, **Friendly (👋)**, **Edgy (🔥)**, and **Fact Check (✅)** modes.
*   **Instant Undo:** A compact mechanism to instantly revert the text replacement if the user changes their mind.
*   **Own Your AI:** A privacy-first model where users provide their own OpenAI API key, ensuring data sovereignty and low cost.
*   **Smart Context:** Automatic reply generation based on clipboard content, reducing cognitive load for incoming messages.

---

## 4. Key Use Cases
*   **The Rushed Professional:** Turning a bulleted list of messy thoughts into a formal status report for Slack or Email.
*   **The Fat-Fingered Mobile User:** Cleaning up clumsy mobile typing while walking or commuting.
*   **The "Angry Draft" Filter:** Drafting a blunt response and letting Echo "translate" it into a polite, effective message.
*   **The Social Networker:** Crafting "Edgy" responses for Twitter or LinkedIn to match the platform's vibe.

---

## 5. UI/UX Vision (General)
*   **Desktop:** A sleek, glassmorphic bar that slides from the bottom or appears near the cursor.
*   **Mobile:** A floating action bubble (Chat Head style) and a custom keyboard row.
*   **Visual Language:** Minimalist, dark-mode first, with neon accents (Cyan for Professional, Peach for Friendly, Purple for Edgy).
